Roadmap

The Truebex roadmap

What we are building next, in our own words. An item joins the feature list only when it ships.

  • Planned
  • In progress
  • Shipped

Work with every file

  • Works with IFC and DWG

    Exchange models and drawings with every other tool on the project.

    Planned

  • Projects from other tools

    Bring in reference scenes and whole projects from the tools your partners use, without a plugin.

    Planned

  • Point clouds, checks and handover

    Point clouds, coordination issues, model checks against requirements, and building data handed over in open formats.

    Planned

Model more of the building

  • Roofs, curtain walls and structure

    Roofs with skylights, glazing systems, columns, beams, foundations, railings, ramps and lifts.

    Planned

  • Site, wall layers and materials

    Topography, layered wall assemblies and a material library with physical properties.

    Planned

  • Clash detection

    Find where elements collide before anyone on site does.

    Planned

  • Building services

    Ducts, pipes and circuits that route and size themselves.

    Planned

Drawings and quantities

  • Elevations, revisions and print sets

    Elevations, legends, keynotes, detail views, revisions, view templates, phases and design options.

    Planned

  • Schedules of everything

    Quantities, bills of quantities and material take-off that update as you draw.

    Planned

Light, comfort and analysis

  • Daylight, energy, sound and wind reports

    Measured results from the one true model, on demand or as you work.

    Planned

Present and share

  • Panoramas, tours and share links

    Panoramas, 360° tours, videos and sun studies, shared with a link that opens on any phone.

    Planned

  • Step inside with a headset

    Walk the model in a PC headset, and see cloud panoramas on a standalone one.

    Planned

  • Room themes

    A whole look for a room, applied in one step.

    Planned

Together, in the cloud

  • Your team on one model

    Several people in one project, with every change in a history you can rewind.

    Planned

  • On your phone and in a headset

    Panoramas rendered in the cloud, refreshed the moment anything changes, and edits from wherever you are.

    Planned

Real products, real prices

  • The model prices itself

    Real products with live prices and stock, by region, so the bill of quantities updates as you draw.

    Planned

Assistant and automation

  • An assistant that edits the model

    Ask for a window on the north wall and it appears, with the light measured and the change undoable.

    Planned

  • Checks and layouts made for you

    Code checks, furniture layouts by room type, and presentation packs prepared automatically.

    Planned

  • Scripts for repeat work

    A script console over the same actions as the assistant, with scripts saved as assets.

    Planned

Account and platform

  • Arabic, right to left

    The whole app in Arabic, with metric and imperial units.

    Planned

  • Sign in, trial and updates

    Sign in from the app, try Pro free for 14 days, and get updates on a stable or beta channel.

    Planned

  • A public API, webhooks and SDKs

    Work with your projects from your own code, with webhooks and ready-made SDKs.

    Planned

  • Feedback and reliability

    Opt-in crash reports, in-app feedback with a screenshot, and a platform that stays up.

    Planned

True Building Experience

The building design platform where daylight is measured, surfaces design themselves and every change is instant.
