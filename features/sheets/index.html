Drawing sheets that lay themselves out

One command lays out the sheet set (cover, plans, sections, stair sheets, schedules and renders) with forms you fill once. Export a vector PDF with selectable text and fillable fields, or DXF.

Download for WindowsCreate free account

How it works

  1. 01

    Run one command

    Truebex creates the sheet set from the model: cover, plans, sections, stairs, schedules and renders.

  2. 02

    Fill the forms once

    Project details go into forms once and appear on every sheet.

  3. 03

    Dimensions keep up

    Auto dimensions stay current as walls and openings move.

  4. 04

    Export

    Vector PDF with selectable text and fillable fields, or DXF.

Dimensions that stay current

Dimension styles for plan, 3D and sheets, auto dimensions that follow the model, relevance per view, and pins that keep the dimensions you choose always visible.

Our own PDF and DXF writers

Truebex writes its own vector PDF: crisp at any zoom, with text you can select and form fields you can fill.

Questions

Which sheets does Truebex create?

+

A cover, plans, sections, the stair sheets, face sketches, renders and schedules, laid out by one command.

Can I export to PDF and DXF?

+

Yes: vector PDF with selectable text and fillable form fields, and DXF.

Do dimensions update when the design changes?

+

Yes. Auto dimensions stay up to date as walls and openings move, and pins keep chosen dimensions visible.

Can I export DWG or IFC?

+

Not yet. DWG and IFC exchange are on the roadmap.
