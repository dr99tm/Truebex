## 1. Purpose of this document

Use this brief to update the Truebex website, product messaging, and brand materials. It captures the project's core idea, chosen slogan, messaging direction, intended audiences, and a proposed homepage copy draft.

## 2. Brand identity

**Brand:** Truebex  
**Meaning:** True Building Experience  
**Chosen slogan:** **Imagine More. Build Better.**

### The idea behind the slogan

- **Imagine More.** Encourage architects, interior designers, and property owners to explore more possibilities, compare alternatives, and refine ideas while changes are still relatively easy.
- **Build Better.** Connect those better-informed decisions to more successful spaces, clearer expectations, and fewer avoidable surprises during construction.

The two halves express a cause-and-effect relationship: **more exploration can lead to better decisions, which can lead to better outcomes.** The slogan is aspirational rather than a list of features. It is short, rhythmic, easy to remember, and broad enough to support Truebex as the platform evolves.

**Suggested Arabic adaptation:** «تخيّل أكثر. ابنِ أفضل.»  
Keep the English slogan as the primary international tagline. Treat the Arabic version as an adaptation; natural wording may be adjusted for the specific context.

## 3. The product vision

Truebex is intended to help designers and owners iterate through design options quickly, make decisions together, and understand a space before construction begins.

The underlying belief is that when people can explore alternatives, compare materials and layouts, evaluate dimensions and quantities, and experience a space at scale, they can make better-informed decisions before those decisions become expensive or difficult to change.

Truebex is not positioned merely as a rendering tool. Its broader promise is to connect the **visual, spatial, and measurable** aspects of designing and developing a space.

### Core workflow

**Explore more possibilities → make better-informed decisions → experience the space before construction → build with greater confidence.**

### Core value proposition

Truebex brings architectural design, real-world materials, measurable project information, and immersive spatial experience into one connected workflow—so people can explore options and make decisions before construction begins.

### What the brand should communicate

- Creativity and freedom to explore alternatives.
- Fast iteration between designers and owners.
- A shared, clearer understanding of the intended space.
- Real-world design context for materials and products.
- Measurable geometry and useful project quantities.
- The ability to experience the design before it is built.
- Better-informed decisions and fewer avoidable surprises.

Avoid promising that a project will be completely problem-free. A more credible promise is **greater clarity, fewer surprises, and more confidence before construction**.

## 4. Primary audiences

### Architects
Develop precise spaces, explore alternatives, and communicate design intent beyond conventional drawings or fixed views.

### Interior designers
Compare layouts, finishes, furnishings, and materials in context; help clients understand and evaluate options.

### Real estate developers
Evaluate design directions, understand proposed spaces earlier, and support more informed project decisions.

### Contractors and engineers
Use measurable geometry and connected project information to support coordination, planning, and early quantity or cost estimation where supported.

### Clients and property owners
Understand the design, compare options with the designer, and experience the proposed space before committing to construction.

## 5. Messaging principles

1. **Lead with the outcome, not the feature list.** Begin with the better decisions and better spaces that the workflow enables.
2. **Keep the language clear and memorable.** Favor active words, short sentences, and specific benefits.
3. **Present Truebex as a connected workflow.** The differentiator is the relationship between design, materials, quantities, and spatial experience—not simply the existence of any one feature.
4. **Show collaboration.** Designers and owners should be able to explore possibilities and make decisions together.
5. **Be ambitious but credible.** Do not imply that software guarantees a flawless build or perfectly accurate final costs in every situation.
6. **Keep the tagline separate from the explanation.** "Imagine More. Build Better." should create the impression; supporting copy should explain how Truebex helps deliver it.

## 6. Slogan exploration and decision

The user's preferred candidates during the exploration were:

- See It Before You Build It
- Make Better Places
- Make It Real
- Beyond the Plan
- Imagine More
- Make Space Real
- **Imagine More. Build Better.**

The chosen direction is **Imagine More. Build Better.** because it balances creativity with real-world outcomes. It is short, has a strong two-part rhythm, and speaks both to the designer's desire to explore ideas and the owner or developer's desire for better project decisions.

Other useful lines may be used as supporting headings or campaign copy, but should not compete with the main slogan:

- **See it before you build.** — a clear supporting message about pre-construction experience.
- **More than a plan.** — useful for explaining that Truebex goes beyond conventional drawings.
- **Better choices. Better spaces.** — a concise summary of the product philosophy.
- **Explore more. Choose better.** — describes the decision-making process.
- **Make ideas real.** — emphasizes the move from concept to space.

## 8. Claims to verify before publishing

Before putting the full copy live, confirm that the current product build supports each advertised capability, especially:

- Real-time or synchronized updates between design elements and related information.
- Integration with a real-world materials or product marketplace.
- Automatic or model-based quantity calculations.
- Cost estimation and the assumptions behind it.
- VR experience and the supported hardware/workflow.
- Team collaboration, APIs, integrations, and on-premises deployment, if offered in pricing plans.

Distinguish calculated quantities from final construction costs, which may depend on local prices, labor, waste, and site conditions. Do not publish pricing or plan features until they have been verified.

## 9. Final recommendation

Keep **Imagine More. Build Better.** as the primary slogan.

Build the website around this message:

> Truebex helps designers and owners explore more possibilities, make better-informed decisions together, and experience a space before construction—so they can move forward with greater confidence.

The slogan should stay short. Use the hero paragraph and the sections below it to explain the product and its practical value.
