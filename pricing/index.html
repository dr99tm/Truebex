Pricing questions

Plans, trials, billing and seats.

Is Truebex free to use?

+

Yes. Free is the full modeller with measured daylight and surface fills, with no time limit. Projects have one storey, PDF and DXF exports carry a watermark, and the asset library comes with Pro.

How does the Pro trial work?

+

Every account can try Pro free for 14 days, once. When the trial ends, your projects stay yours and keep opening on Free.

What is the difference between monthly and annual billing?

+

It is the same plan, paid every month or once a year. A year paid at once costs less than twelve monthly payments.

Can I pay for Team monthly?

+

No. Team is billed once a year, per seat. The monthly figure shown is that annual price divided by twelve.

Which currencies can I pay in?

+

Prices are shown in pounds sterling, US dollars and euros. Choose yours above the plans.

What does per seat mean?

+

Team is priced per person, from 2 seats. Each seat runs Truebex on up to 2 computers.

What are founding seats?

+

The first 300 founding seats on Pro and Team keep 30 % off annual plans for as long as the subscription stays active. The offer ends on 31 January 2027.

Can I cancel at any time?

+

Yes. A paid plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, and your projects stay yours on Free.
