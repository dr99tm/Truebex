Frequently asked questions

Short answers about Truebex, hardware, the API and billing.

What is Truebex?

+

Truebex is building design software for Windows. Architects and interior designers use it to explore design options, compare materials and finishes in context, check dimensions and areas, experience the space at full scale and produce the drawings — all from one connected model.

How is the lighting in Truebex different?

+

Each window and door measures the sky and sun outside it and lets that exact amount of light into the room. Move a window, widen a door or add a wall, and the light in every room responds — including rooms that only receive light through another room.

What are dynamic surface fills?

+

Patterns that understand the surface they sit on. You set the rules — what goes at the start, the end, the centre or in between — and Truebex lays them out, re-flowing them whenever the wall or its openings change.

What happens when I edit an asset?

+

Open any saved asset, change it, and save. Every copy placed across the project updates in a single step, so a detail is only ever designed once.

What do I need to run Truebex?

+

A Windows PC with a modern graphics card. A card with hardware ray tracing unlocks the highest lighting quality, including ray tracing and path tracing.

Is there an API for developers?

+

Yes. Create API keys in your dashboard and call the Truebex API with a bearer token. Every plan includes a monthly request allowance; see the developer docs.

Does Truebex work with IFC and DWG files?

+

Today Truebex exports vector PDF and DXF. IFC and DWG import and export are on the roadmap, together with sharing a project with your team.

How can I pay?

+

Pro is billed monthly or annually by card, and Team annually per seat. You choose a plan and pay from Billing in your dashboard: VAT or sales tax for your country is shown before you pay and on your invoice. You can cancel renewal at any time. When online payment isn't available, Billing says so and how to reach us.
