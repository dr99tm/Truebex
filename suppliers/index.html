For suppliers

Sell your products on Truebex

Truebex is where architects and interior designers draw rooms and see their daylight measured. The supplier portal is open: apply, build your catalogue with prices and stock for every region you deliver to, and keep it current from a spreadsheet or a feed.

Apply as a supplier

You apply with a Truebex account: sign in, or create one, on the next page.

What you get

  • Your catalogue

    Products with their variants, dimensions in millimetres, materials, pictures and one 3D file per variant: GLB, glTF, OBJ, FBX or a Truebex object, up to 100 MB. Every 3D file is checked as it uploads.

  • Prices and stock by region

    A price list for every region you deliver to, in its own currency, with tax, delivery cost and days, stock and lead time. Set the stock or change the price of many rows at once.

  • Spreadsheet and feed import

    Fill in the spreadsheet template, or send CSV or JSON. A dry run shows what would be created, updated or rejected before you apply it, and a feed address you register is read every day.

  • Leads, quotes and orders in one inbox

    Answer a request for quote with your prices and a message. Accept an order, mark it shipped with the carrier and reference, then delivered. A buyer's contact shows only on your own leads and orders.

  • Numbers on your products

    Search impressions, product views, placements, requests for quote and orders, per product, region and day. Counts only: no buyer is named.

  • Your team and your systems

    Invite colleagues as owner, catalogue editor, order handler or viewer, and give your own systems a supplier API key for feeds. Every page works on a phone as well as at a desk.

How onboarding works

  1. 01

    Apply with your company

    Send your company details, the regions you deliver to, a contact and one company document: a PDF of at most 10 MB.

  2. 02

    We review it by hand

    The first suppliers are onboarded one by one. A person at Truebex checks your company and your document, then e-mails you the decision.

  3. 03

    Prepare your catalogue

    While we review, build your range in the portal: import it, set your regional prices and fix any row the dry run rejects.

  4. 04

    Every product is reviewed

    Once you are verified, submit your products. We review every product before it is listed.

On the roadmap

The portal is open today. These parts of the marketplace are being built; the roadmap shows their status as they move.

  • On the roadmap

    Your products inside Truebex

    Designers will find your products in the catalogue inside the app, place them in the room they are drawing at their true size, and see your price for their region.

  • On the roadmap

    A cost that updates as designers draw

    Your prices will flow into the project's live cost, so a product will be costed the moment it is placed.

  • On the roadmap

    Orders paid on Truebex

    Card payments for orders will open once the marketplace terms are signed off. Until then every supplier is quote-only: you answer requests for quote, and sell and invoice on your own terms.

Questions

Who can sell on Truebex?

+

Companies that make or sell furniture, lighting, kitchens, bathrooms, paint, wallpaper or tiles; that can sign a contract and invoice as a company; that deliver in at least one region with their own price list there; and that hold the rights to their product pictures and 3D files.

Do I need 3D files?

+

Yes, for every object you sell: one file per variant, in GLB (preferred), glTF, OBJ, FBX or as a Truebex object, up to 100 MB. Finishes such as paint, wallpaper and tiles need no 3D file.

Can my catalogue stay current on its own?

+

Yes. Register a feed address and Truebex reads it every day, or send files from your own systems with a supplier API key. Every run leaves a report of the rows created, updated, unchanged and rejected.

Can designers pay by card?

+

Not yet: card payments for orders are on the roadmap. Until they open, you answer requests for quote, and sell and invoice on your own terms.

Can my team use the portal?

+

Yes. Invite colleagues by e-mail, each with a role: owner, catalogue editor, order handler or viewer. Every page works on a phone as well as at a desk.
